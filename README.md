## 🇨🇳 中文

面向网文创作全流程：写或优化开篇、第一章、前三章、章节正文、大纲、设定、人设、CP、情节桥段、简介、导语与投稿文。也能做网文市场调查、分析相似作品、拆解公开作品、模拟网文编辑审稿，并制定连载规划。覆盖女频、男频、短篇、长篇、爽文、甜宠、悬疑、玄幻、末世、无限流等任务。不用于范文批量入库、小说素材库维护、小红书运营或非小说类写作。

- 简介、导语、投稿文：直接产出可投递文案
- 市场调查：分析相似网文与题材热度，定位卖点
- 编辑视角审稿：模拟网文编辑挑毛病，给出改稿方向
- 连载规划：排布卷章节奏与更新大纲

## 🇬🇧 English

A full pipeline for web-fiction writing: craft or polish openings, the first chapter, the first three chapters, chapter bodies, outlines, worldbuilding, characters, CP pairings, plot beats, blurbs, teasers and submission copy. It also runs market research on web-fiction genres, analyses comparable titles, breaks down publicly available works, simulates an editor's manuscript review, and plans a serialisation schedule. It covers female-led and male-led channels, short and long form, power-fantasy, sweet-romance, suspense, xianxia, apocalypse and infinite-flow genres. It is not for bulk reference ingestion, material-library upkeep, social-media operations or non-fiction writing.

### ✨ What it does

- Opening and first-three-chapters polish: rewrite the hook and first conflict for retention
- Worldbuilding, characters and CP pairing: fill in settings, relationships and payoff chains
- Blurbs, teasers and submission copy: produce ready-to-send text
- Market research: analyse comparable web novels and genre heat to locate the selling point
- Editor's-eye review: simulate a web-fiction editor's critique and give revision directions
- Serialisation planning: lay out volume/chapter pacing and an update outline
